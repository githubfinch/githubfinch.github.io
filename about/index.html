What have I been doing?

Before heading off to undergrad, I was fully prepared to be studying illustration or fine art, and even had a portfolio going. But I was also incredibly absorbed by the natural sciences, so against the advice of most, I switched focus last minute, and went to study physics at the University of Manchester.

Early on in my undergraduate years, I read Frank Herbert's God Emperor of Dune, and marveled at the prospect of being able to predict how a system will behave based on sufficient knowledge of present and historical data. That system can be anything, a flock of birds, a group of voters, or a bunch of cells. On top of that, I also wanted to understand how a system of identical individuals following a few simple rules is capable of generating complex patterns. Following that line of thought, I took courses like statistical mechanics and non-linear dynamics, and enrolled in the course complex systems modelling at Kings College London.
